* **Key Takeaway:** Instant visual wow; establishes this as the real operating model of the whole company.

## Scene 04: Journey 1 — Sale to Cash

* **Visual State:** Camera frames the Revenue zone. A bright transaction pulse travels from Lead → KYC Verification → Unit Selection → Discount Approval → Contract E-Sign → Installment Schedule → Automated Receipt → Bank Reconciled → GL Posting.
* **Presenter Action:** Point to the Discount Approval node (shows ruby gate), then to Contract (shows document icon), then to General Ledger (amber glow).
* **Spoken Script:**
  > "Let us trace the complete life cycle of a unit sale in Mazar Mall:
  > 
  > A lead registers through an agent. KYC documents are uploaded to our secure document vault. The client selects Unit 402. 
  > 
  > If the sales manager offers an 8% discount, the system halts: workflow automatically routes to the Executive for approval before the reservation can finalize.
  > 
  > Once approved, the contract is generated. An immutable installment schedule is locked into Accounts Receivable.
  > 
  > When the customer pays their down payment via bank transfer, the bank feed matches the receipt, settles the installment, posts a double-entry journal, updates the customer statement, and immediately alerts executive management that Unit 402 is secured."
* **Presenter Action:** Advance to Scene 05.

## Scene 05: Journey 2 — Procure to Pay

* **Visual State:** Dual-track visualization showing the physical material flow (blue lines) synced with the financial commitment and liability flow (amber lines).
* **Spoken Script:**
  > "Now observe procurement and cost control:
  > 
  > A site engineer requests 50 tons of structural steel. The system checks the BOQ and current budget before creating the Purchase Requisition.
  > 
  > An RFQ goes to approved vendors; bids are compared side-by-side. Once the Purchase Order is issued, an accounting **Commitment** is registered against the project budget.
  > 
  > When the steel arrives at the gate, QA conducts inspection. The Goods Receipt Note (GRN) is signed. The system debits Inventory and credits Goods-Received-Not-Invoiced.
  > 
  > When the supplier's invoice arrives, an automated **3-way match** verifies PO, GRN, and Invoice line-by-line. If quantities and prices match within tolerance, it releases to Accounts Payable for scheduled payment. Zero leakage. Total budget discipline."
* **Presenter Action:** Advance to Scene 06.
