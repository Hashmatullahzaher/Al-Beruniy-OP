Worked Examples — same engine, different policies

Purchase Request

Purchase Order
Proc Mgr → FM ≤T1 → CFO ≤T2 → CEO >T2 · AI bid comparison attached
Expense
Employee → Dept Mgr → Finance → CFO · AI duplicate flag · Telegram receipt intake
Customer Discount
Agent → Sales Mgr → Sales Dir → CFO (by %) · AI margin impact
Sales Contract
Sales Mgr → Legal → Finance → Sales Director
Contractor Payment (IPC)
QS → Site Eng → PM → Finance → CFO · Telegram notice → deep link
Supplier Payment
AP → FM ≤T1 → CFO ≤T2 → CEO >T2 · never approvable inline from Telegram
Payroll
HR Officer → HR Mgr → Finance → CFO · no AI or Telegram action path
Budget / Revision
PM → Project Dir → CFO → CEO · AI EAC explanation attached
Change / Variation Order
Site Eng → PM → Project Dir → CFO · AI may draft
Segregation of Duties is enforced across all entry channels: the person (or AI acting for a person) who creates cannot approve or pay.
