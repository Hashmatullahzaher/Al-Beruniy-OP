Telegram Identity Binding

2 · Requests Telegram linkone-time code · QR · deep link

4 · TelegramIdentityBinding createdTelegram user ID + chat → Type-A User Account

Telegram username ≠ trusted identity. Binding is revocable from ABOS. Group membership is never authorization. Telegram is a channel — not a Business Party, not a role. BP-23
AUTHORITY & DATA-ACCESS EXAMPLES — identical for web, AI Copilot and Telegram
Finance Manager
Approves ≤ T1; posts JEs; AI can explain any variance in own entity; Telegram payment approval → deep link + step-up.
Chairman
Read-all + consolidated AI briefing; approves top tier; no data entry — via web or Telegram.
Project Manager
Assigned projects only; AI answers & Telegram alerts limited to those projects.
Sales Agent
Own customers only; asking the AI “who is at risk?” returns only own accounts; no payroll, no GL.
Storekeeper
Creates stock transactions; AI may draft a reorder; cannot approve payments anywhere.
HR Officer
Employee records; AI masks salary/ID fields; payroll run needs HR Mgr + Finance.
AI Platform Admin
Providers, models, quotas, health — no business data unless separately granted.
AI Knowledge Steward
Approves knowledge sources & classification; cannot read Restricted content it classifies.
Telegram Bot Admin
Bot, webhook, channels, delivery ops — cannot impersonate or read as business users.
Auditor
Read-only everything incl. AI run audit & Telegram correlation logs; zero write.
