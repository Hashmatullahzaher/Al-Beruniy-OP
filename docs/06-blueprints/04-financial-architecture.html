THREE ACCOUNT TYPES — NEVER CONFUSED
A · USER ACCOUNT
Login/authN only. Employee, portal supplier, portal customer. No money.
B · BUSINESS PARTY
Customer / Supplier / Contractor / Employee master. Holds relationship & balances-by-party.
C · LEDGER ACCOUNT
Cash·Bank·AR·AP·Revenue·Expense·Asset·Liability. The GL.
Link: one Party (B) may have one User (A) for portal access and maps to a control Ledger account (C) (e.g. Customer → AR control). Sub-ledger keeps per-party detail; GL keeps the control total.
