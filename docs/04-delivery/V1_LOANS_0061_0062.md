# V1 loans: migrations 0061 and 0062

The audit and the architectural decision are in `docs/03-architecture/V1_LOAN_UNIFICATION_AUDIT_AND_DECISION.md`.

Not deployed. Production stays at 0060 until the owner authorizes these.

## What was built

- **Sources:** `LOAN_RECEIPT` and `LOAN_REPAYMENT`, on the existing posting kernel, reversal mechanism, FX snapshots and Safe guard.
- **Records:** `abos.loans` and `abos.loan_repayments`. Each row is written once and changes only by its single decision. Neither table stores a balance.
- **Accounts:** a new control `LOAN_PAYABLE` beside the existing `SHAREHOLDER_LOAN`. A shareholder loan credits `SHAREHOLDER_LOAN` and every other loan credits `LOAN_PAYABLE`.
- **Lender:** an existing `business_parties` row, or a new one with role `LENDER`. A shareholder keeps their one party.
- **Approval:** workflow type `LOAN`. OFF or unset posts directly. ON holds the record for a second person.
- **Permissions:** `finance.loan.read`, `record`, `repay` and `approve`. `approve` is independence-enforced, so only a Super Administrator can grant it.
- **Screens:** Add Money, Loans with a per-loan statement, and a Loans section on the shareholder screen.
- **Temporary funding:** sources marked `compatibility_only`. Its page stays reachable by URL and has left the menu.

## Journals

| Event | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Shareholder capital 50,000 | Treasury 50,000 | Shareholder Capital 50,000 (equity) |
| Shareholder loan 20,000 | Treasury 20,000 | Shareholder Loan Payable 20,000 |
| Other loan 30,000 | Treasury 30,000 | Loan Payable 30,000 |
| Repayment 5,000 | Loan Payable 5,000 | Treasury 5,000 |
| Reversal of that repayment | Treasury 5,000 | Loan Payable 5,000 |
| AFN loan 7,000 at 70 | AFN Safe, USD 100 | Loan Payable, USD 100 |

## Rules worth knowing

- A repayment cannot exceed what is outstanding, counting repayments still waiting for approval.
- A loan cannot be reversed while an unreversed repayment exists.
- Repayments and reversals of one loan take the loan row's lock, so they run one at a time.
- A repayment is in the loan's own currency. Cross-currency repayment is refused.
- Interest is not in scope. No requirement for it exists.

## Reports

Loan receipts raise Treasury and liabilities, repayments lower both, and neither touches income or expense. The Trial Balance, General Ledger, Account Statement, Safe Statement and Income Statement needed no change, because they read posted journals. The Balance Sheet, Financial Summary and Shareholder Statement were taught the two sources.

## Confidentiality

Lender identity, source ids, references, notes and links need `finance.loan.read`, or `finance.temporary-funding.read`, which already grants lender visibility. A report reader keeps the amounts, dates, liability totals and generic labels. A reversal of a loan follows the same rule through 0060.

## Before use in production

After deployment a Super Administrator gives the Finance role the four new permissions, and someone creates the two liability accounts. No migration grants a permission or creates an account.

## OWNER DECISION REQUIRED

### 1. An AFN loan repaid at a different rate

**Question:** when an AFN loan is repaid at a different rate from the one it was received at, what happens to the USD difference on the liability?

- **Option A, leave it visible (current behaviour).** The native loan clears to zero. The liability keeps the USD difference, and the loan statement says so. It clears when a future FX settlement source exists.
- **Option B, repay at the loan's own rate.** The liability clears exactly in both currencies, but the Safe's USD value then moves at a rate that is not the day's rate.
- **Option C, post a gain or loss.** This needs a new FX settlement source, which you excluded from this slice.

**Accounting effect:** native balances are identical in all three. The liability and Treasury USD values differ by the rate difference. Nothing is hidden: both sides of each journal balance.

**Recommendation:** Option A for now, and build Option C as its own slice when AFN loans are actually used. Production has no AFN Safe today.

### 2. A lender who pays a bill directly

**Question:** should a lender paying a company bill directly be a Loan?

- **Option A, leave it out for now.** Temporary funding's direct-payment mode has no replacement in the Loan lane. It has never been used.
- **Option B, add it.** A later slice adds Dr Expense, Cr Loan with no Treasury movement.

**Accounting effect:** none today. Under Option A, such a payment cannot be recorded as a loan until it is built.

**Recommendation:** Option A until the case occurs.
